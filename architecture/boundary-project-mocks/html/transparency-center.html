Internal readiness vs the transparency projection

one internal readiness → many entangled projections, worldwide
Internal readiness is the superset. A supplier's internal readiness spans more frameworks than any single transparency view. Each buyer / regulator sees only a projection (tunable omission) — and the same internal readiness is projected into lots of different entangled projects, to lots of different demand parties, across a global footprint (states, countries). Transparency data is repetitive by design: it reuses the crowd's assessment logic.

Readiness mesh

reflexive assessment
The crowd is itself assessed. Every assessor's workstation is inside the ZB platform boundary and continuously assessed — and so is every transparency-marketplace participant. Security of the mesh is guaranteed by making the mesh part of the assessed boundary. Fractal: the assessors of the supply chain are themselves in the supply chain.
